About NirmaanHub

NirmaanHub is a construction directory for Indian cities. Homeowners compare local builders, run a square-foot cost calculator, and send enquiries. Construction companies list for a minimal yearly fee. Every form on this website is stored locally and emailed to our support desk.

What we publish

  • Local builder cards with rating, years, GST and starting ₹ / sq ft.
  • Category images with approximate cost bands and build duration.
  • A calculator that applies a city rate factor, package inclusions, add-ons, 7% contingency and 18% GST.
  • Company listing from ₹499 / year after a review of the form you submit.

Figures on this site are indicative. Always confirm a detailed BOQ, drawings and statutory approvals with the contractor you hire.
